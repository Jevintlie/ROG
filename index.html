01 / Overview

A gaming machine that learned how to travel.

The original G14 challenged the idea that serious laptop performance required a large chassis. Its pitch was a deliberate balance: an eight-core mobile processor, dedicated graphics, colour-accurate display options, and a body starting at 1.6 kg.

This redesign turns that balance into the visual system. Dense specifications sit inside a spacious editorial layout, while electric cyan and signal red identify the moments that deserve attention.

05 / Portfolio rebuild

From coursework upload to intentional front-end work.

The first version proved that I could assemble a multi-page HTML/CSS experience. This rebuild focuses on what matters in professional front-end delivery: purpose, hierarchy, accessibility, maintainability, and honest product framing.

Self-directed brief

Keep the hardware story.
Replace the borrowed presentation.

The original submission became raw material rather than a template. Every page, claim, and interaction had to earn its place in the rebuild.
