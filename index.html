CPU


The AMD Ryzen 7 4900HS is the fastest laptop processor tested in multi-threaded workloads, it’s also the fastest in single-thread performance. The 4900HS is 35% faster than the 8-core Core i9-9880H when using all cores, and 7% faster in single-core. The chip is manufactured in the modern 7 nm process at TSMC with 8 cores and 16 threads. The 4900HS integrates all eight cores based on the Zen 2 microarchitecture and support SMT / Hyperthreading.
